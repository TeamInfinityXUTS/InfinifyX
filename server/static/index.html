📖 How to Use

Smart Drive · Road Guardian Guide

1

Allow Camera Access

When prompted, grant the browser camera permission. The system uses your camera to detect road objects in real time.

2

Click Start Detection

Press the Start Detection button on the Home screen. Frames are streamed to the AI inference server automatically.

3

Read the Risk Dashboard

The right panel shows the current Risk Level (Low / Medium / High), a 0–100 risk score, and the estimated distance to the nearest front vehicle.

4

Bounding Box Colours

Detected objects are highlighted by class: Blue = vehicles · Purple = person · Red = traffic light · Orange = bike/motor.

5

Voice Alerts

When risk reaches Medium or High, a spoken warning plays automatically. Toggle voice in Settings.

6

Stop Monitoring

Click the red ⏹ Stop button at the top to end the session and return Home.

Risk Levels Explained

Low (0–33)
Road is clear. Drive normally.
Medium (34–66)
Potential hazard ahead — stay alert.
High (67–100)
Immediate risk detected — slow down!
