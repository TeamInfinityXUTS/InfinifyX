📖 How to Use

Smart Drive · Road Guardian Guide

1

Allow Camera Access

When prompted, grant the browser camera permission. The system uses your device camera to detect road objects in real time.

2

Tap Start Detection

Press the Start Detection button on the Home screen. The camera activates and frames are streamed to the AI inference server automatically.

3

Read the Risk Dashboard

The monitoring screen shows your current Risk Level (Low / Medium / High), a 0–100 risk score, and the estimated distance to the nearest front vehicle.

4

Bounding Box Colours

Detected objects are highlighted: Blue = car/bus/truck   Purple = person   Red = traffic light   Orange = bike/motor.

5

Voice Alerts

When risk reaches Medium or High, a spoken warning plays automatically. Toggle voice in Settings.

6

Stop Monitoring

Tap the red ⏹ Stop button at the bottom of the monitoring screen to end the session and return Home.

Risk Levels Explained

Low (0–33)
Road is clear. Drive normally.
Medium (34–66)
Potential hazard ahead — stay alert.
High (67–100)
Immediate risk detected — slow down!
